Portfolio – Privacy policy

For the Android game “Portfolio: Island Money Game”. Last updated: 15 October 2026. Latviski

In short

Portfolio collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, it asks for no permissions, and it sends nothing over the internet.

What stays on your device

Your saved game, the family profiles (the names, colours and pictures you choose, levels, statistics and achievements) and your settings are kept only in the game's own private storage on your device. The developer never receives them.

If Android's backup is turned on, Android may include these files in your own Google backup, so that they move with you to a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account; the developer cannot see it.

Children

Portfolio is made for families and for ages 10 and up. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game, or open Android's Settings → Apps → Portfolio → Storage → Clear storage. That deletes everything the game kept.

Changes

If a later version adds online play, this page will say first exactly what is sent, why, where and for how long it is kept – before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the e-mail address on the game's Google Play page.
